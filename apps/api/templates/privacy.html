Zero Tracking Guarantee
No cookies, no user profiles, no telemetry tracking.

1. Information We Collect

ClipGlass operates without user registration or accounts. We only temporarily process the media URL or file that you directly submit for transcoding, audio filtering, or trimming. We do not inspect personal profiles or store behavioral histories.

2. How We Use It

Submitted media streams are utilized exclusively to execute the requested operational tasks (e.g. ffmpeg encoding, modular audio filter chains, or sentence-aligned auto-trimming). We never train machine learning models on your files, nor do we sell, share, or monetize any user data.

3. 15-Minute Ephemeral Storage Purge

All temporary download files, converted audio waveforms, rendered vertical short clips, and logs are housed in an isolated ephemeral directory. A continuous background worker permanently unlinks and overwrites these assets after 15 minutes of inactivity.

4. Your Rights & Browser-Local Session

Your session history is stored locally in your browser's localStorage and never sent to external telemetry servers. You can immediately wipe all local download tokens and session memories at any time by clicking "Clear Session" in the Recent Session Drawer.

5. Contact & Security Inquiries

For questions regarding privacy, security hardening, or ephemeral data policies, review our open documentation or submit inquiries via our documentation and FAQ center.
