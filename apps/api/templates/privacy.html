Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Ephemeral media processing with zero permanent storage.

1. What We Collect

We only process the media URL or file you directly provide to perform transcoding, trimming, or audio filtering. We do not require accounts, logins, email addresses, or personal profiles.

2. How We Use It (15-Minute Ephemeral Lifecycle)

Submitted media streams are utilized solely to execute the requested conversions or filter chains. All temporary files and waveforms are permanently deleted by a background sweeper after 15 minutes of inactivity.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or monetize user data. No external advertising networks, trackers, or behavioural analytics are installed.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

ClipGlass uses browser localStorage only to remember your UI theme preference (Dark/Light) and local session drawer history. These tokens never leave your local device.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about our ephemeral storage policy or privacy design, review our technical documentation or reach out via our support channels.
