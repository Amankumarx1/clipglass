Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ClipGlass audio mastering, video trimming, and formats.

Why does ClipGlass recommend "Original" for audio instead of MP3 320?

YouTube streams native audio in AAC (128 kbps) or Opus (160 kbps). Converting these to MP3 320 kbps creates a larger file without adding acoustic fidelity. Selecting "Original" preserves the bitstream bit-for-bit without lossy recompression.

How long are my processed files stored?

All extracted videos, mastered audios, and trimmed shorts are strictly ephemeral. Files are automatically unlinked and permanently purged from server memory/disk after exactly 15 minutes.

What are the processing duration and file size limits?

Videos up to 3 hours in length and up to 4K resolution (2160p60) are fully supported. For lossless trimming and stream copying, exports finish almost instantaneously regardless of size.

Can I download private, live streams, or members-only videos?

No. In compliance with security standards, ClipGlass only processes publicly accessible media and active stream feeds.

Are there any ads, pop-ups, or user accounts?

Never. ClipGlass has zero third-party tracking scripts, zero redirects, zero banner ads, and requires no registration or login.
