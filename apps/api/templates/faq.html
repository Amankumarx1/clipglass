Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about ClipGlass workflows and privacy.

🔍
How do I download from YouTube? ▼

Paste any YouTube or YouTube Music link into the Downloader search bar, click Inspect, select your desired resolution (up to 4K) or audio format (MP3, FLAC, WAV), and click Download.

Is it legal to use ClipGlass? ▼

ClipGlass is designed for processing personal media, public domain files, Creative Commons recordings, and transformative editing permitted under fair-use doctrines. Users are responsible for complying with the copyright status of external media.

What file formats are supported? ▼

ClipGlass supports MP4 (H.264 / AV1) and WebM (VP9) for video up to 4K 60fps, as well as MP3, M4A, Opus, FLAC, and WAV up to 320 kbps for audio.

How long does processing take? ▼

Trimming using stream-copying completes in under 1 second without re-encoding. Audio effect rendering takes 2 to 6 seconds. All downloaded and processed media are automatically purged from the server after 15 minutes.

How does Auto Mode differ from Custom Mode in Audio Studio? ▼

In Auto Mode, you pick effects and intensity (Subtle, Balanced, Strong), and ClipGlass configures optimal parameters. In Custom Mode, you access a full modular rack with sliders for every parameter.
