Why does ClipGlass recommend "Original" for audio instead of converting to MP3 320? ▼

YouTube streams native audio in AAC (128 kbps) or Opus (160 kbps). Converting these to MP3 320 kbps creates a larger file without adding acoustic fidelity. Selecting "Original" preserves the bitstream bit-for-bit without lossy recompression.

How does the 79-Module Audio Studio work in Auto vs Custom Mode? ▼

In Auto Mode, you pick whichever creative and mastering effects you want (e.g. 8D Audio, Deep Bass, Shimmer Reverb), and ClipGlass automatically calculates harmonious parameters, gain staging, and True-Peak safety limiting. In Custom Mode, you have a modular rack with sliders and knobs to adjust every single parameter manually.

How long are my processed files stored on the server? ▼

All extracted videos, mastered audios, and trimmed shorts are strictly ephemeral. Files are automatically unlinked and permanently purged from server memory/disk after exactly 15 minutes.

What makes the Video Trimmer "lossless"? ▼

ClipGlass uses stream-copying (-c copy) with keyframe-accurate cuts when possible. This means the video stream is not re-encoded, avoiding generational quality loss and completing exports in under 1 second.

How does the Auto Clip viral shorts algorithm work? ▼

Auto Clip analyzes multimodal signals: energy volume peaks, pitch shifts, speech transcript sentiment, and speaker activity. It scores segments from 0-100 on Hook, Flow, Engagement, and Trend, selects optimal 15-60 second moments, and formats them in 9:16 vertical cards with karaoke-style captions.
