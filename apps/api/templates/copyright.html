1. Policy Overview

ClipGlass is an ephemeral media processing software tool. It is designed to assist creators, editors, and researchers with transformative audio editing, trimming, and format transcoding. ClipGlass does not host, curate, or maintain a public searchable library of copyrighted works.

2. Filing a DMCA Notice

If you are a copyright owner or authorized agent thereof and believe that any content made available through or processed by our services infringes upon your copyright, you may submit a formal notification under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512(c)) containing:

  • A physical or electronic signature of the authorized agent.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  • Identification of the specific URL or resource located on the service.
  • Your contact information (address, telephone number, and email address).
  • A statement that you have a good faith belief that the disputed use is unauthorized.
  • A statement made under penalty of perjury that the information in the notification is accurate.

3. Fair Use & Transformative Works

Under Section 107 of the Copyright Act, allowance is made for "fair use" for purposes such as criticism, comment, news reporting, teaching, scholarship, and transformative research. Users applying effects, remixing, or excerpting short analytical clips bear sole responsibility for verifying their legal protections under relevant fair use doctrines.

4. Repeat Infringer Policy

ClipGlass reserves the right to block domains, IP addresses, or automated agents that repeatedly attempt to use the platform for unauthorized distribution of copyrighted media in violation of international intellectual property treaties.
